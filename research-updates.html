Evidence is typed, not a universal ladder: a sequence prediction, structural simulation, domain annotation, transcript and measured phenotype answer different questions. Multiple computational checks are not automatically independent experimental confirmation.

R01

CORE STUDY

Deep learning reveals antibiotics in the archaeal proteome

Torres, Wan and de la Fuente-Nunez | 2025-08-12 | Peer-reviewed

Application: Direct archaeal sequence application with selected experimental follow-up. Peptides mined from archaeal proteomes; activity tested against bacterial pathogens.

Evidence supports: The study mined 233 archaeal proteomes and prioritized 12,623 candidates; 75 of 80 selected synthesized peptides were active against at least one tested bacterial strain.

Do not extend this to: The hit rate concerns a selected test set, not all predicted candidates. Mouse-model evidence is not human clinical efficacy or proof of a natural antimicrobial role in archaea.

Your audit question: Which denominator does the reported hit rate use, and which claim would require a new experiment?

Read depth: Metadata, abstract and selected Results passages. Locator: Results: Deep-learning-guided identification; Antimicrobial activity; abstract.

Read the primary source: 10.1038/s41564-025-02061-0

R02

CORE STUDY

Histone diversity in the archaeal domain of life

Laursen and Luger | 2026-04-15 | Peer-reviewed | Version of record dated 2026-06-12

Application: Direct archaeal computational analysis. Archaeal histone sequence diversity across GTDB release 220.

Evidence supports: The study combines archaeal histone clustering with structural predictions and simulations; some predicted nucleosome-like arrangements are unstable in simulations.

Do not extend this to: Prediction plus simulation is still computational evidence. It does not establish how the proposed assemblies organize DNA in living archaeal cells.

Your audit question: Are two computational analyses independent experimental confirmation? What biological observation is still missing?

Read depth: Metadata and selected Results, Discussion and Methods passages. Locator: Results: structural prediction; Fig. 5; Discussion; Methods: Structural prediction.

Read the primary source: 10.1038/s41467-026-71849-3

R03

CORE STUDY

Genome modelling and design across all domains of life with Evo 2

Brixi and colleagues | 2026-03-04 | Peer-reviewed

Application: General model with a reported archaeal evaluation component. Multi-domain genomic data, including archaeal gene-essentiality benchmarks.

Evidence supports: The paper reports zero-shot gene-essentiality predictions evaluated against experimental labels across bacterial, archaeal and phage species.

Do not extend this to: An archaeal benchmark is more than training-set inclusion, but it is not evidence that generated archaeal genomes are viable or that every lineage and task generalizes.

Your audit question: Which evidence supports an archaeal prediction task, and which broader design claim remains unsupported?

Read depth: Metadata, selected Results and figure-caption passages. Locator: Fig. 2j; gene-essentiality Results paragraph; Extended Data Fig. 3h.

Read the primary source: 10.1038/s41586-026-10176-5

R04

CORE STUDY

Jumbo circular extrachromosomal elements of methane-oxidizing archaea with variably extensive metabolic and defense gene repertoires

Shi and colleagues | 2026 | Peer-reviewed

Application: AI-assisted archaeal annotation within an integrated biological study. Methanoperedens and associated extrachromosomal elements.

Evidence supports: The study combines genomic and expression evidence for archaeal extrachromosomal elements and deposits custom ColabFold/AlphaFold3 models alongside its data.

Do not extend this to: A predicted fold, a transcribed gene and a measured metabolic effect are different evidence objects; do not infer a change in methane turnover from a model alone.

Your audit question: What establishes host association, and what additional evidence would establish an encoded gene's physiological effect?

Read depth: Selected primary HTML passages and PDF abstract/data-availability passages. Locator: Abstract; Results: ECE-host association; Data availability.

Read the primary source: 10.1038/s41467-026-74423-z | Publisher PDF

R05

CORE STUDY

Comparative analysis of adhesin-like proteins from Methanobacteriales species

Tan, Gupta, Er and Seedorf | 2026-09-29 | Peer-reviewed

Application: Direct archaeal structural-domain annotation. Methanobacteriales, including intestinal and rumen lineages.

Evidence supports: The paper maps adhesin-like protein architectures across 17 species and describes curated model-assisted domain recognition.

Do not extend this to: Proteins exceeding 5,000 residues were excluded from prediction. PRISM is described as a manuscript in preparation; its executable release was not verified here. Domain labels do not prove adhesion mechanisms.

Your audit question: How do sequence-length filtering, reference annotations and split design limit a biological claim?

Read depth: Metadata, abstract and selected Methods/Discussion passages. Locator: Methods: Sequence retrieval and structural modeling; Characterization of ALP domains; Discussion.

Read the primary source: 10.3389/fmicb.2026.1897686

EXTENSIONS

Useful transfer, bounded claims

R06 / Rapid and accurate prediction of protein homo-oligomer symmetry using Seq2Symm

Seq2Symm was applied to the Pyrococcus furiosus proteome as part of a multi-proteome analysis.

Boundary: Proteome-scale predictions are not independent physical validation of every archaeal oligomer.

Read depth: Metadata and selected Results passages.

10.1038/s41467-025-57148-3

R07 / A realistic phantom dataset for benchmarking cryo-ET data annotation

The phantom benchmark links tomograms and curated annotations; the authors explicitly acknowledge incomplete labels and possible false positives.

Boundary: Curated ground truth is not absolute, and performance on a phantom does not establish archaeal cellular segmentation or molecular identity.

Read depth: Metadata and selected Results/Methods passages.

10.1038/s41592-025-02800-5
