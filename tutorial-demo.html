TUTORIAL DEMO 01 · LITERATURE TRIAGE

Build an evidence brief,
not just an answer

Use three synthetic archaeal records to practice question framing, transparent retrieval, evidence extraction, and human verification. Everything runs locally in your browser.

Training boundary

This demo uses a deterministic keyword scorer, not a language model. The records are synthetic and have no DOI. The transparent mechanism lets you inspect the workflow before connecting real literature or an external AI service.

HUMAN REVIEW CHECKPOINT

Before reusing the brief

  • Are the organisms assigned with a current and stated taxonomy?
  • Does the evidence show genomic potential, expression, activity, or a measured rate?
  • Is the environmental context comparable to the target question?
  • Could the same marker support a different pathway or interpretation?
  • Have primary sources replaced all synthetic or secondary training material?
