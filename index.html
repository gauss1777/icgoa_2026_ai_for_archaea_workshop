01 / ORIENTATION

AI is useful only when the evidence chain remains visible.

Archaeal research spans fast-changing taxonomy, incomplete genomes, environmental context, and specialized metabolic inference. A fluent answer is not enough. This workshop starts with the research question, preserves provenance, and places human verification at every consequential step.

02 / LEARNING PATH

Three moves, one auditable workflow

  1. A

    Ground the biology

    Review archaeal cell biology, major metabolisms, habitats, taxonomy, and common misconceptions.

    Open the starter knowledge base
  2. B

    Retrieve candidate evidence

    Translate a biological question into explicit concepts and inspect why each record was retrieved.

    Run Tutorial Demo 01
  3. C

    Build and challenge the brief

    Attach evidence sentences to claims, record uncertainty, and identify the checks that still require expertise.

    Read the tutorial notes

03 / OUTCOMES

What learners should leave with

QUESTION

A bounded research question

Specific enough to determine which evidence, metadata, and exclusions matter.

EVIDENCE

A traceable evidence table

Source IDs, supporting sentences, relevance logic, and unresolved uncertainty remain connected.

REVIEW

A human verification plan

Taxonomy, function, environment, causality, and recency are checked before reuse.
